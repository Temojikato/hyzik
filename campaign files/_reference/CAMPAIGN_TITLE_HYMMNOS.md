# Campaign title — canonical Hymmnos rendering

## Display forms

- Common: **The Tower: A Dance of Song and Chaos**
- Hymmnos source/script: `tonelico : grlanza oz hymmnos en Chaos`
- Romanized pronunciation: **toneliko : glantza oz hyumnos en Chaos**

The Hymmnos source line becomes the script form when rendered with the campaign's `Hymmnos` font. The underlying source remains canonical lexicon text; the display font supplies the Hymmnos glyphs.

## Literal ledger

| Token | Lexicon role | Attested meaning | Title use |
|---|---|---|---|
| `tonelico` | noun, unmarked | divine tree; possible alternate meaning “tower” | The Tower |
| `grlanza` | verb, Central | dance | dance/dancing, used nominally in this compressed non-executable title |
| `oz` | preposition, Central | of; possessive article | of |
| `hymmnos` | noun, Central | song; Hymmnos | Song |
| `en` | conjunction, Central | and; because; for | and |
| `Chaos` | campaign proper noun | the cosmological core of Omnia | Chaos, intentionally retained unchanged |

## Chaos decision

The campaign lexicon contains no direct Hymmnos word meaning **Chaos**. `dazua` means darkness, `ksyura` means destruction/ruin/collapse, and `tussu` means change. Each can be used as imagery *caused by* or *associated with* Chaos, but none is an accurate name for the campaign's central cosmological concept.

Therefore **Chaos remains the proper noun `Chaos`** in the Hymmnos title. This follows the authoring protocol's proper-noun rule and avoids silently reducing Chaos to darkness, destruction, or change. It is an intentional Common proper noun embedded in a Hymmnos title, not a claimed lexicon entry.

## Execution status

This is a nominal campaign title, not executable Song Magic. It therefore does not require an Emotion Sound or a complete Song Magic clause. `grlanza` is a poetic title-form use of the attested verb “dance”; it must not be treated as evidence that the lexicon contains a separate noun “dance.”
