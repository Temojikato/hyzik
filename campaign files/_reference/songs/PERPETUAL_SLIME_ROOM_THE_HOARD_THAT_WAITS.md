# PERPETUAL SLIME ROOM — The Hoard That Waits

Status: Eleven Music v2 six-minute exploration-room draft, lexically audited 2026-07-18.

## Canonical basis

- The Avatar of Greed and Patience is the only discovered Avatar of a current or ancient Deity of Chaos and may be among the oldest Avatars.
- It is the progenitor of Slimes and important to both the Tower and the town's survival.
- It may communicate with all its offspring, but this is unconfirmed and must not be presented as fact.
- Its treasure-filled pods appear to punish taking through consuming Slimes and may reward those who leave treasure, according to Diver belief.
- It seems to show no visual sentience. It collects, sheds, remains eternal, and does not move.
- Its aura saps will, inspires fear and awe, and causes hesitation.

Sources: `src/dataSets/avatars.js`; Perpetual Slime Room regions in `src/mapdata.ts`.

## Narrative function

This is six-minute room ambience, not a dedicated combat phase. The Avatar does not sing. One ancient Tower-facing Reyvateil system voice performs a warning/observation in Hymmnos. The song deliberately avoids confirming that the Avatar communicates through its offspring.

Greed is represented by accumulation: sounds are acquired and denied decay. Patience is represented by an unmoving central tone that never pursues the listener. Everything else circulates, approaches, is consumed, or is returned.

## Music prompt — under 4,000 characters

```text
Create a six-minute quiet, ancient, eerie dark science-fantasy ambience titled “Perpetual Slime Room — The Hoard That Waits.” The room contains the Avatar of Greed and Patience: an impossibly old, apparently mindless, unmoving progenitor of Slimes amid treasure pods. Divers believe taking treasure makes its offspring consume them, while leaving treasure may earn a later reward. Never confirm that belief.

Use about 46 BPM in heavy 4/4 and E-flat Phrygian with cold whole-tone crystal colors. Build from one unmoving sub-bass/crystal fundamental, glass harmonics, wet granular movement, viscous low synth, tiny gold-like chimes, distant relays, bowed metal, membrane creaks, and sparse prepared piano. Keep it spacious beneath dialogue. No conventional groove.

The central fundamental represents the Avatar and persists almost unchanged: it never approaches, chases, reacts theatrically, or signals sentience. Arrange eight related but nonidentical pod motifs around it in stereo, like offspring sharing an inaudible pressure. Never use creature voices or confirm telepathy.

Make greed an acoustic law. Chimes accumulate and refuse to decay; the mix hoards pitch and resonance. Make patience equally dangerous: changes take long enough that listeners lean toward them. When crowded, a soft wet subtraction consumes one layer and leaves a rarer, brighter tone. The room never hurries. Silence feels watchful.

Use phase-coherent liquid clicks, trapped pitch drift, time-stretched droplets, source-less reversed attacks, glints in the wrong pod, and brief will-sapping subharmonic pressure. Avoid comic slime, cauldron, or busy squelch sounds.

VOCAL RULE: Use one bright artificial female-coded Reyvateil-like system songstress—an ancient warning, not the Avatar's voice. Remain instrumental before 3:35. From 3:35 to 4:40, sing all supplied lines once, in order, as one continuous cold observation. Use a narrow through-composed melody with patient gaps that never divide the passage. Never repeat, omit, reorder, echo, or reprise text. Add no words or meaningless vowels. No whisper, speech, second singer, choir, chorus, hook, or other vocal. Keep consonants clear.

0:00–0:55 — THE UNMOVING CENTER. Establish the fundamental, eight pod positions, and almost no upper detail.

0:55–1:55 — ACCUMULATION. Add one treasure-like resonance at a time. Each remains too long. The center does nothing.

1:55–2:50 — TEMPTATION. Bright objects seem close enough to touch. Harmonic approach produces low pressure and delayed wet shadows, never an attack.

2:50–3:35 — CONSUMPTION WITHOUT MOTION. Pods remove accumulated layers. The center remains still; consequences arrive through its surroundings.

3:35–4:40 — THE BARGAIN. Perform all lyrics once. Stolen gold drains warmth and thickens the pod field. Gold left behind creates a small silence. Time's reward introduces one pure high tone of uncertain benevolence.

4:40–5:25 — SHEDDING. Instrumental only. The pure tone splits into offspring motifs while the center remains unchanged. Suggest relation, never confirmed communication.

5:25–6:00 — PERPETUAL RETURN. Remove nearly everything except the fundamental and one unresolved golden chime. End near the opening for looping, as if no time passed.

No battle climax, heroic melody, sentimental beauty, lush orchestra, pop structure, EDM, trailer percussion, jump scare, loud attack, fast ostinato, comic slime effects, random language, or vocals outside the defined passage.
```

## Translation ledger

| Function | Hymmnos | Conservative literal meaning | Contextual reading |
|---|---|---|---|
| Greed | `Ma ki wa knawa rre kurt marta syast omni.` | In stable focus that I accept, I know the ancient mother gathers everything. | The Avatar accumulates without needing to hunt. |
| Progenitor | `Ma ki wa knawa rre kurt marta pomb vallne.` | In stable focus that I accept, I know the ancient mother creates offspring. | The Avatar is the progenitor of Slimes. |
| Patience | `Ma num wa knawa rre kurt marta teyys yanje.` | In stable emptiness that I accept, I know the ancient mother remains forever. | Its patience is indistinguishable from geological permanence. |
| Taking | `Was paks ga knawa yorr stel kiala.` | In intense nervousness that I want ended, I know you steal gold. | Removing treasure is treated as theft by the warning system. |
| Consequence | `Was paks ga knawa rre vallne eta yor.` | In intense nervousness that I want ended, I know offspring eat you. | Slimes consume those who take from the pods. |
| Restraint | `Ma zweie ra knawa yorr iqwayes kiala.` | In stable determination that I want continued, I know you leave gold behind. | The Diver resists immediate reward. |
| Patience/reward | `Ma touwaka ra knawa rre gfine accrroad rannef yor.` | In stable hope that I want continued, I know Time sends reward to you. | Waiting may eventually be rewarded—or Divers merely believe it will. |
| Hoard | `Ma ki wa knawa rre kurt marta haf omni.` | In stable focus that I accept, I know the ancient mother holds everything. | What is taken, left, shed, and consumed all returns to the same hoard. |

`kurt marta` literally means “ancient mother.” It is a contextual title for the Avatar as progenitor, not a claim about sex, gender, intelligence, or self-identification. `marta` is the sole Ancient Metafalss word in the passage, chosen deliberately for an archaic progenitor title. `omni` is used nominally in its attested “everything/all” sense.

## Validation notes

- Every lyric token occurs exactly in the campaign lexicon.
- All words except the deliberately archaic `marta` are Central Standard Note or dialect-unmarked.
- Every line has a complete Emotion Sound, a speaker verb, and a correctly marked external subject via `rre` or subject-form `yorr`.
- There are no direct local headwords for greed, patience, slime, pod, or hoard. The lyric uses attested actions and relationships instead of inventing labels.
- The warning distinguishes lore from rumor: it presents the take/consume and leave/reward rule as the room's cautionary bargain, while the music prompt explicitly refuses to confirm the belief's truth.
- Nothing in the lyric claims that the Avatar sees, thinks, speaks, or communicates with its offspring.
