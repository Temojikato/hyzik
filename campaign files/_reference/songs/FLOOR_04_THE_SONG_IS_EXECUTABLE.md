# FLOOR IV — The Song Is Executable

Status: Eleven Music v2 six-minute exploration-ambience draft, lexically audited 2026-07-18.

## Narrative function

- Tutorial lesson: Hymmnos is executable Song Magic, not merely a language placed over music.
- Demonstration: the song targets the room, requests a voice, changes system mode, protects the room, reveals light, stops the light, and halts.
- Hidden explanation: Standard Hymmnos lines state that words become magic, feelings enter the song, the song executes magical power, and feelings alter that power.
- Musical role: an ancient compiler hymn—sacred, technical, eerie, and quietly dangerous.
- Thesis: vocabulary specifies an operation, while the singer's Emotion Sound supplies parameters that can change what the operation becomes.

## Generation method

Use a single six-minute generation. Paste the prompt into Eleven Music's prompt field and the plain eleven-line block into its custom lyric field. Do not add headings, character labels, translations, or directions to the lyric field.

All vocals form one continuous passage performed by one singer. The alternating registers are differences in phrasing, not different characters: server packets are clipped and syllabic; explanatory Hymmnos is more melodic.

## Music prompt — under 4,000 characters

```text

It is an ancient compiler performing a sacred lesson nobody can yet translate.

Use about 64 BPM, restrained 4/4, and D Dorian with a rigid six-note lattice. Build from phase-locked clocks, glass sine tones, relays, low machine drones, metallic resonators, prepared-piano impulses, granular data flicker, and precise synthetic bells. Keep it spacious beneath dialogue. Add restrained bit errors, clipped tails, checksum repetitions, microtonal disagreement, and short buffer dropouts. Chaos corrupts syntax and causality, not merely volume.

Give four concepts distinct signatures: TARGET is a centered low pulse; INPUT is sudden empty space; MODE CHANGE rotates the lattice into a new timbre; EXECUTION makes the named state real. Reuse them consistently enough to be learned.

VOCAL RULE: Use one bright artificial female-coded songstress with precise Reyvateil-like articulation and an unnatural formant. Remain instrumental before 2:35. From 2:35 to 3:55, she performs every supplied line once, in order, as one uninterrupted compiler hymn. Never repeat, omit, reorder, echo, or reprise text. Add no words or meaningless vowels. Do not whisper, speak, change character, add a choir, or create a chorus.

Within the passage, alternate two phrasings without changing singer. ADDR, DIA, chmod, and HALT lines are clipped one- or two-note server packets. Pronounce them “ah-dur,” “dee-ah,” “chee-mohd,” and “halt.” Other lines are intelligible melodic Hymmnos on narrow evolving phrases. Do not split the passage into sections.

Make the arrangement obey the lyrics. ADDR targets the room. DIA creates a brief input-shaped gap beneath the voice. chmod changes timbre and harmonic orientation. Protection locks the clocks into a resonant enclosure. Reveal-light adds clear upper harmonics; stop-light removes them. The feelings-transform-magic line recolors an earlier motif through its Emotion Sound. On HALT, voice and clock stop instantly; after a short silence, only the instrumental system returns.

0:00–0:50 — BOOT. Relays awaken and establish the lattice. Pitch checks travel through stereo.

0:50–1:40 — TOKENIZATION. Motifs separate, undergo inspection, and join like words in a parser. No voice-like sounds.

1:40–2:35 — PARAMETERS. Repeat one neutral three-note operation with different harmony, pressure, and decay, implying emotional input changes it.

2:35–3:55 — COMPILER HYMN. Perform all lyrics once. Every command produces its audible consequence. Nothing is translated into Common.

3:55–4:45 — EXECUTION PROOF. Instrumentally reconstruct target, input, mode change, protection, light, darkness, and stop.

4:45–5:30 — SYNTAX CORRUPTION. Chaos swaps cause and result: echoes precede attacks, a response arrives before its prompt, and one lattice tone becomes invalid. The system detects contradictions and recompiles. Remain quiet and eerie.

5:30–6:00 — CLEAN EXIT. Restore clock and lattice, leaving one unresolved input pulse for a future singer. End loop-compatible.

No English narration, heroic melody, lush orchestra, pop chorus, EDM, trailer percussion, jump scare, loud climax, repetitive vocal hook, random language, or vocals outside the defined passage.
```

The four command packets use dictionary-attested server-control commands. They are code packets, not claimed examples of ordinary Standard Hymmnos sentences.

## Validation notes

- Every lyric token occurs exactly in the campaign lexicon, including the case-sensitive server commands.
- All natural-language words are Central Standard Note or dialect-unmarked.
- Every executable or explanatory Standard sentence has a complete three-part Emotion Sound.
- `rre` correctly marks `wart`, `asyar`, or `hymmnos` as the external subject of the explanatory statements.
- `sosar` is deliberately selected because its note identifies spellcasting/sorcery rather than magic as a mysterious miracle.
- `hymme` provides the nested action “sing/resonate” beneath `exec`; the following verb compound supplies the intended operation.
- The explanation lines vary their openings to reduce accidental musical refrains while preserving a coherent technical lesson.
