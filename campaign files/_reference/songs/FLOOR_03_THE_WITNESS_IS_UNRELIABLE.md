# FLOOR III — The Witness Is Unreliable

Status: Eleven Music v2 six-minute exploration-ambience draft, lexically audited 2026-07-18.

## Narrative function

- Tutorial lesson: trust nothing—not a friend, enemy, stranger, or even oneself.
- Threat model: the floor does not merely lie; it destroys the dependable relationship between a warning and what follows it.
- Musical role: quiet paranoid exploration ambience in which safe and hostile cues repeatedly exchange meanings.
- Diegetic vocal source: one ancient-system Reyvateil voice delivering one contiguous diagnostic hymn.
- Thesis: Chaos presides over truth and betrayal without favoring either. Suspicion is useful, but it cannot become certainty.

## Generation method

This floor deliberately uses only one singer and one contiguous vocal passage. Multi-character delivery, scattered micro-vocals, whisper acting, and non-lexical vocal instructions are reserved for encounters worth editing manually, especially bosses.

## Music prompt — under 4,000 characters

```text
Create a six-minute quiet, eerie dark science-fantasy dungeon ambience titled “Floor III — The Witness Is Unreliable.” This protected tutorial floor teaches: trust nothing—not a friend, enemy, stranger, or yourself. Chaos presides over trust and betrayal equally. The music is an unreliable narrator; no sound permanently means safe or dangerous.

Use about 58 BPM, restrained 4/4, centered on F-sharp minor but unsettled by major thirds, tritones, and microtonal detuning. Build the ancient system from two precise clock pulses, low glass drones, distant relays, muted synthetic bells, metallic resonance, prepared-piano taps, and a cold three-note verification motif. Keep it spacious, quiet, menacing, technological, and suitable beneath dialogue. Add restrained buffer glitches, clipped reverb tails, phase errors, reversed transients, false echoes, and brief dropouts. No large climax.

The two clocks represent ancient protection and begin synchronized. Corruption makes one anticipate or lag, but the system forces them together. Make evidence contradict itself: sometimes an ominous motif leads to safety; sometimes the warmest chord introduces corruption. An echo answers with one wrong note. Stereo reflections imply movement from the wrong direction. Never establish “pretty equals safe, dissonant equals danger.”

VOCAL RULE: Use exactly one bright artificial female-coded virtual songstress, Reyvateil-like, with precise synthetic articulation and a slightly uncanny formant. The track is fully instrumental before the single vocal passage. Around 3:50, she sings all supplied lines exactly once, in written order, as one continuous cold diagnostic hymn lasting roughly 45–60 seconds. Use a narrow through-composed melody that changes with every line; no chorus and no repeated melodic hook. Keep every word intelligible. Do not whisper, speak, change singer, split into characters, harmonize into a choir, sustain meaningless vowels, hum, add words, repeat a line, echo a word, reorder text, or reprise lyrics. After the final line, the voice stops permanently and the remainder is instrumental.

0:00–0:55 — CALIBRATION. Both clocks agree. Present the verification motif with sterile confidence, but let one reflection contain a nearly inaudible wrong pitch.

0:55–1:50 — FALSE WARNING. A threatening low pressure and tritone approach, then reveal no attack. The system restores calm as though it detected nothing.

1:50–2:45 — FALSE SAFETY. A warmer version of the verification motif appears trustworthy, then quietly sheds beats, detunes, and places echoes in impossible locations. Containment corrects it without triumph.

2:45–3:50 — THE DOUBLE. A second instrument copies the lead almost perfectly, alternately helping and sabotaging it. Reverse which layer seems original. Let the listener lose confidence in the first clock, then the second.

3:50–4:50 — DIAGNOSTIC HYMN. One synthetic songstress delivers the complete supplied lyric block once. Under her, friend/enemy and truth/betrayal share the same harmonic material. The system remains controlled, but her pitch develops tiny digital fractures near the inward-facing final line.

4:50–5:35 — CONTRADICTION FIELD. Instrumental only. Exchange the roles of every established cue: the threat texture stabilizes the clocks while the verification motif corrupts them. Keep this subtle and quiet.

5:35–6:00 — FALSE RESET. Return close to the opening texture for looping, but the two clocks now disagree by a tiny interval even when their rhythm aligns. End with two equally plausible resolutions sounding together; neither is confirmed.

No heroic melody, lush orchestra, pop structure, EDM drop, trailer percussion, jump scare, loud combat peak, repetitive ostinato, random language, or vocals outside the one defined passage.
```

| Function | Hymmnos | Conservative literal meaning | Contextual reading |

| No moral cue | `Ma ki wa anturn rre dazua haf cest en deggeez.` | In stable focus that I accept, I confirm darkness holds truth and betrayal. | Chaos does not consistently favor honesty or treachery. |

`dazua` literally means darkness. “Chaos” is the campaign-context interpretation of darkness holding both sides, not a claimed dictionary translation.

## Validation notes

- Every non-metadata token occurs in the campaign lexicon.
- All words are Central Standard Note or dialect-unmarked.
- Every sentence uses a complete three-part Emotion Sound and an attested speaker verb.
